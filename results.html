Benchmark results

Theorem formalization is measured on the 143 problems of Formal TCS Bench (statements from recent TCS papers). A submission succeeds only if it compiles and passes independent semantic review. Proof formalization uses 248 theorems from the PFR and TestingLowerBounds (TLB) projects in RLMEval; success means a Lean proof with no sorry. Each model is run as direct generation, inside Codex CLI, inside Claude Code, and inside Turing Agent. All numbers are pass@1 with one final submission per problem.

Pass@1 (%). Underlined: best in column. PFR covers additive combinatorics and entropy methods (Polynomial Freiman–Ruzsa); TLB covers information-theoretic lower bounds for hypothesis testing.

With GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Turing Agent reaches 57.34% and 61.54% success on Formal TCS Bench — 16.78 and 32.17 points above Claude Code, the strongest baseline — with compilation rates of 97.20% / 99.30%. The gap between compiling and being faithful is exactly why semantic review is evaluated separately.

On RLMEval it exceeds 82% overall with both models: 82.66% with GLM-5.3 (best of all methods, 2.42 points above Claude Code) and 82.66% with DeepSeek-V4-Pro, 8.06 points above Codex CLI and within 4.03 points of Claude Code.
